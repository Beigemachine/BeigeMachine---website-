DISKSNAP

Compact backups for SD cards and USB drives.

For Windows · Free

Download DiskSnap How it works

WHAT IT DOES

  • Only the used space

    DiskSnap images hold the data on a drive, not its empty space, so a mostly empty card makes a small image.

  • Restore to smaller drives

    Restore an image to a smaller SD card or USB stick, as long as the data fits.

  • Back up, restore, clone

    Save a drive to a .dsnap image, restore it later, or clone one drive straight to another.

  • Careful with your disks

    System and internal drives are protected, and nothing is overwritten without a clear confirmation first.

  • Works offline

    Everything runs on your PC. No account, no cloud, no uploads.

  • Free to use

    Backing up, restoring and cloning are free.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. 1

    Plug in

    Insert the SD card or USB drive you want to work with.

  2. 2

    Choose

    Pick Backup, Restore or Clone, then choose the source and target.

  3. 3

    Snap

    DiskSnap does the rest and checks with you before anything is overwritten.
